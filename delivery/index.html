How to play

Hold Left / Right (A / D) or drag sideways: steer
Hold Up / Down (W / S) or drag up / down: accelerate / brake
Space or THROW: a package at the nearest car ahead.
On a phone or tablet there are on-screen buttons for all of it (the menu can switch them on or off).
Reach the finish before the clock hits zero to keep the tip. After zero the tip drains for 10 seconds, and the level is failed.
Hit a spinning green target beside the road with a package for TANK RAGE: you wreck whatever you touch, and Space fires the cannon.
The shoulders are free of traffic, but stay too long and the police lift you off.
Police cars (white, flashing lights) bust you for bumping them, or for shoulder driving while they are near. Three busts and it's over.
In Asteroid Run the ghosts come one after another: catch each before the last runs out and you pass straight through the rocks.
Pickups: cyan turbo, white ghost (drive through things), orange wrench (repair), pink inflatable passenger (shoulders are legal).
Giant frogs hop about on parts of the road; hitting one is like hitting a barrier.
On the bridge the shoulders are solid steel: arrive on the shoulder and you are wrecked.
A signed exit leaves from the right shoulder onto a two-lane side road with oncoming traffic; it rejoins the expressway further on.
Left lanes are oncoming traffic. A head-on wrecks you, and a new car costs time.
Some levels are one-way: in Big Business and Hurricane everything goes your way, and in All Heck everything comes at you.
In Hurricane the cars blown through the air can't touch you; the junk sliding about the road can.
